645: /// [OPUS-4.8] (sq-ebii) [`update_in_place`] under a cooperative [`crate::QueryBudget`].
646: ///
647: /// The budget is installed thread-locally for the whole update, so a `DELETE/INSERT … WHERE`
648: /// whose WHERE pattern blows up is bounded EXACTLY as a budgeted query's `SELECT` is: the
649: /// cross-product allocation is capped up-front (`budget::cap_alloc`) and the evaluation

651: /// of running the writer thread to an OOM. The non-WHERE operations (INSERT/DELETE DATA,
652: /// CLEAR/DROP/CREATE/LOAD) do not consult the budget — they are bounded by their operand
653: /// size, which the request body-size limit already caps — so an unlimited budget (the
654: /// default, via [`update_in_place`]) is byte-for-byte the previous behaviour.
